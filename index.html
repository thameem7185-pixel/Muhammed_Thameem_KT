About me

Who I am, where I'm coming from and how to reach me.

I'm a developer who enjoys turning ideas into working products. I build AI chat interfaces, responsive web apps and Wi-Fi enabled ESP32 projects.

I'm Muhammad Thameem KT, a passionate developer based in Kozhikode, Kerala, India, who thrives on turning creative ideas into functional, polished products. My journey spans across three exciting domains: Web, AI, and IoT. Whether I'm engineering sleek, responsive web interfaces, building futuristic AI chat terminals like Nexus and Aetheron, or writing code for ESP32 microcontrollers in a browser-based IDE,I love bridging the gap between digital software and the physical world. I'm a natural problem solver who is always learning, currently expanding my skills in JavaScript, Node.js, and hardware integration. For me, the best part of development is taking a concept from the initial spark of an idea all the way down to the final line of code and the microcontroller that brings it to life.
