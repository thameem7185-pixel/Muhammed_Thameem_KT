I'm Muhammed Thameem KT, a developer from Kozhikode, Kerala, and I build things people can actually use: AI chat apps, browser-based tools and Wi-Fi enabled ESP32 projects. What drives me is the moment an idea leaves my head and starts working on a screen, or on a board in my hands.

I'm at my best where software meets hardware. In the same year I can build a chat assistant with voice replies and themes, an Arduino IDE that runs in a browser tab, and a dashboard that streams motion data from a sensor over Wi-Fi. Few people are comfortable moving between the interface and the microcontroller, and I enjoy working in both.

I learn by building. I started with Arduino and simple circuits in 2023, moved into HTML, CSS and JavaScript in 2024, and went from small experiments to finished projects that anyone can open on GitHub Pages. That pace comes from curiosity and from never settling for "it works": I keep adjusting spacing, motion, colour and detail until it feels right.

I also work well with modern tools. I build alongside Claude, and I bring what no tool can supply: the idea, the taste and the patience to test every version myself. Describing exactly what I want, spotting what is off and pushing for a better result is a skill, and it is why my projects keep getting sharper.

  • Ships real work. Nexus, Aetheron AI and the Arduino Web IDE are live and open to anyone.
  • Interface to microcontroller. Comfortable across the front end, AI chat tools and ESP32 hardware.
  • Eye for detail. Polished layouts, smooth motion and small touches that make a page feel finished.
  • Fast, driven learner. Three years from first circuits to full apps, and still speeding up.
